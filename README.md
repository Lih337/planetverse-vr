## 📖 Overview

Textbooks and slides often fail to engage learners or help them picture planetary characteristics. PlanetVerse is a VR simulation prototype that puts students inside immersive 3D environments where they can explore **all eight planets** and observe terrain, atmosphere, gravity and habitability.

- **Problem:** students struggle to visualize planetary science with 2D resources, which leads to shallow memorization.
- **Target users:** middle school, high school and university students; teachers; museums and science centers.
- **Why VR:** it makes abstract concepts tangible and lets learners take part actively instead of passively.
- **Innovation:** the project grew from a single planet (Mars) to the **entire Solar System**, so learners can compare planets (for example atmosphere and gravity differences).

---

## ✨ How It Works

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| Main VR environment | A Solar System hub where users select and travel to any of the eight planets |
| User role | Explorer/student navigating and interacting with planetary features |
| Core interaction | Selecting planets, observing features, reading or listening to descriptions, completing quizzes |
| Support materials | On-screen guides, interactive prompts, feedback dashboards |

Each planet loads into its own VR environment with terrain, atmosphere and interactive points of interest.

## 🧗 Challenges

- **Technical complexity:** moving from Mars to eight planets meant many 3D environments and more asset optimization.
- **Performance:** some lag on lower-spec devices because of the number of models and textures.
- **Accessibility:** few VR headsets in schools made testing harder.
- **Time management:** UI, 3D modeling, programming and report writing had to fit the same timeline.
- **Team coordination:** clear communication and scheduling were needed.

## 💡 Lessons Learned

- Immersion boosts motivation and curiosity.
- Comparing planets side by side deepens understanding.
- VR projects must balance realism with performance.
- Dividing tasks clearly and tracking progress helps meet deadlines.
- The framework can be expanded to moons and dwarf planets.

## 🔮 Future Improvements
